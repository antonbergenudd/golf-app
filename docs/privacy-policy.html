Fairway Chaos Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Fairway Chaos (“the App”) is provided by Anton Bergenudd (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it when you use the App.

Information we collect

  • Display name — the name you enter when you host or join a round. The App does not require an account, email, or password.
  • Game content — lobby names and codes, scores, cards, challenges, verifications, and related in-round activity needed to play with the people in your lobby.
  • Technical data — basic service logs needed to operate and secure the App (for example errors handled by our providers).

How we use information

  • To run Fairway Chaos (lobbies, live rounds, cards, scores, and challenge verification).
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.
  • To communicate with you about the App when needed (for example support).

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Game content is visible to other players in the same lobby or round. We use service providers to run the App:

  • Supabase — database and realtime sync for lobbies and games.
  • Expo / Apple — building, distributing, and delivering the iOS app.

These providers process data on our behalf to operate the App. They may process data in the EU/EEA or other regions according to their own infrastructure and agreements.

Data retention

Round data is kept so you can finish and resume a game. Old unused lobbies may be removed automatically. To request deletion of data associated with your display name, contact us at the email below.

Your choices

  • You can choose which display name to use for each round.
  • You can leave a lobby or end a round from the App.
  • You can contact us to request deletion of stored game data.

Children

Fairway Chaos is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
